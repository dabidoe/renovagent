Why this is not
a chatbot wrapper
A chatbot gives you an answer. This gives you a project: persistent state, code-grounded checks, deterministic money math, and a paper trail. Remove the chat and the system still plans, prices, sequences, vets, and exports. Remove the system and the chat is just opinions.


Meta-model architecture (target)
• Llama-class reasoning — Dwang orchestrates; Pete/Sparky/Archie return schema-validated JSON findings only.
• Multimodal intake — room photos + measurements become structured facts with source + confidence; concealed conditions stay UNVERIFIED.
• Tool calling — models call the deterministic engines (BOM, dependencies, permits, vetting score), never invent arithmetic.
• Segmentation (SAM-class) — fixture/floor region masks to sanity-check dimensions (stretch).
• Dwang commits — specialists propose; one orchestrator writes state, with a revision log.
