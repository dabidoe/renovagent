# Retail reference prices

Checked by hand on October 30, 2026. Source: `fixtures/catalog/retail-reference-2026-10.ts`, which generates this table. These are real products with their listed shelf prices in USD. They are not live offers, and they carry no promise of local stock. Tax, shipping and delivery are excluded.

## How the records are stored

`npm run seed:catalog` imports them into Mongo in three layers. The store skips records that are identical to ones already there, and refuses any record that reuses an existing ID with different content.

- **Product** (`catalog_products`): manufacturer, model and only the specs the product page or spec sheet states. A spec that isn't published is left out. The constraint matcher treats a missing spec as a rejection, so such a product is listed but never picked for a requirement that depends on that spec.
- **Offer** (`retailer_offers`): the product at one retailer and SKU, with its URL. The pack quantity is the coverage per pack: sq ft per gallon or carton, lin ft per piece, or `ea`.
- **Observation** (`price_observations`): the dated price. `source: manual-entry` and `freshness: recent`; never `live`. The note names the page and how the price was seen:
  - **page**: the product page was fetched and the price read from it.
  - **search result**: the retailer blocked automated fetches (Home Depot, Lowe's, Ferguson, SupplyHouse and PlumbersStock all returned 403/429), so the price is from a search result for that product page.

A later re-check adds a new observation for the same offer, which builds price history instead of overwriting.

**Tiers are Renovagent's classification.** Builder means the common store brand; mid means a recognised mid-range line. Retailers don't label tiers.

**Load one set per database.** Tier plans take every product in a category, so mixing these real records with the fictional demo fixtures would blend their prices. `--set retail` is the default; `--set demo` loads the fictional fixtures. `--retire-demo` deletes exactly the demo fixture records, by ID, before importing. Projects priced against the demo set keep their old materials list until they are re-priced.

## Choices made with the data

- **Coverage ranges:** where a paint states coverage as a range, the low end is used.
- **Conflicting prices:** where two search results disagreed, the higher price is used, so best-value savings are never overstated.
- **Dropped records:**
  - the Bruce Time Honored engineered hardwood: its price unit wasn't labelled, and it was on clearance with no stock;
  - a BrassCraft polymer supply line: its source was a category page, not the product page.
- **Not floatable:** Lifeproof engineered hardwood and two Floor & Decor engineered lines were found, but they are glue- or nail-down only, so they would fail the floating-install requirement.

## Gaps and rule findings

- **Missing tiers:**
  - no mid-tier 31 in single-hole vanity top was found (the Floor & Decor 31 in tops are drilled for 8 in widespread faucets);
  - no high-tier engineered hardwood, vinyl plank or drain;
  - engineered hardwood has a single product.
- **The faucet rule:** the bathroom recipe first required a faucet with a `1/2-fip` connection. Of four real single-hole faucets, only the Pfister LG142-0600 (½ in IPS shank) met it; Moen Genta and Delta Trinsic connect with 3/8 in compression lines, the common case. Recipe v3 accepts either. Its supply-line requirement says that a faucet with attached 3/8 in lines connects straight to the stops and needs no separate lines. Delta stays out because it was out of stock, and Glacier Bay because its connection isn't published.
- **Products listed but never picked:**
  - the HOMEIBRO vanity: mount type isn't published;
  - the Glacier Bay faucet: connection isn't published.
- **Transolid quartz top:** it has an undermount basin, not a molded sink.
